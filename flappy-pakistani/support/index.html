For help with Flappy Pakistani, email tsl.ctproducts@gmail.com.

Helpful details to include

Please include your device model, Android or iOS version, app version, and a short description of what happened. A screenshot can help. Do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

The game has no sound

Use the speaker icon in the upper-right corner, then check your phone's media volume.

How do I turn music on or off?

Use the music-note icon in the upper-right corner. Your choice stays on your device.

My best score disappeared

Best scores are stored only on your device. Reinstalling the game or clearing app data resets them.

How does Share score work?

Share score opens your phone's system share sheet after a game ends. Nothing is sent unless you choose a destination and complete the share yourself.
