FLAPPY PAKISTANI

Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026 · App ID: com.ahmediqbal.flappypakistani

Summary

Flappy Pakistani is a one-touch game that works without an account. The current release build does not collect, sell, or share personal information and does not use analytics.

Information kept on your device

The game keeps your best score, sound and music choices, and a coarse age-band choice on your device. It does not ask for a date of birth. These settings are not sent to us. Clearing app data or uninstalling the game removes them.

Sharing a score

After a game ends, you may choose Share score. The game gives your phone's system share sheet a PNG score card and score text. You choose whether to share it, which app receives it, and who sees it. Flappy Pakistani does not automatically post, message, or transmit the score card.

Advertising and networks

The current release configuration does not initialize or request advertising or consent services, and it does not show ads. Debug builds used during development may use Google sample advertising identifiers solely for local testing; they are not the public release. If a later release adds advertising or other data practices, this policy and applicable store disclosures will be updated before that release.

Permissions

The game does not request camera, microphone, contacts, photos, notifications, precise location, or account access. Android includes internet capability for platform compatibility, but the current release game does not operate a player-data server.

Website and contact

These policy pages are hosted with GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary technical request data under the GitHub Privacy Statement. For support or privacy questions, email tsl.ctproducts@gmail.com or visit the Flappy Pakistani support page.

Changes

Any material change to the game's data practices will be reflected on this page before the affected release is published.
