BIRD MISBEHAVING?

We can help

Desi Udaan should be simple: tap, flap, laugh, repeat. If it is doing something stranger, tell us.

Open a support request

Quick fixes

The game has no sound

Tap the speaker button in the top bar, then check that your phone is not muted and its media volume is turned up.

My best score disappeared

The best score is stored only on your device. Clearing app data, reinstalling, or changing devices resets it.

Urdu text looks clipped

Please include your device model, operating-system version, and a screenshot in your support request.

The bird will not flap

Tap anywhere inside the game area. If the game is paused, close the tea-break card first. Restarting the app can also help.

How do I delete my data?

Desi Udaan stores only language preference and best score. Clear the app’s storage or uninstall it to remove both.

When reporting a problem, include: device model, Android/iOS version, app version, selected language, and what happened. Please do not post passwords or other private information.
