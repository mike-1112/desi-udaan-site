English

Desi Udaan is published by Ahmed Iqbal. This policy distinguishes the original release from the upcoming advertising update.

Version 1.0.0 and the web game

The original version 1.0.0 and the web game have no advertising SDK, accounts or analytics. They do not collect or share personal information. The language preference and best score stay on your device.

iOS and Android version 1.1.0 advertising release

When installed, version 1.1.0 uses Google AdMob to show ads between games. Gameplay works offline and no account or purchase is required. This update is not available until approved and released on the relevant app store.

Google may collect IP addresses (used to estimate approximate location), device or app identifiers, ad views and interactions, product interactions, crash logs, and performance or diagnostic information. Google uses this data to deliver and measure ads, maintain its services, and prevent fraud. Advertising and measurement providers may receive data for these purposes. See Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s iOS SDK data disclosure and Android SDK data disclosure.

Your choices

The app requests non-personalized ads and does not request access to the iOS advertising identifier through an App Tracking Transparency prompt. Non-personalized ads still involve data processing. Where required, Google’s consent form appears before ad requests. Open the app information panel using the logo to access Advertising privacy options when available in your region. You can change or withdraw consent there. If consent cannot be obtained or ads cannot load, the game remains playable without ads.

Local data and permissions

Your chosen language and best score are stored only on your device; clearing app data or uninstalling removes them. The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, photos, notifications, or precise-location access. Ads need an internet connection. Android may use vibration for gameplay feedback.

Audience and retention

Desi Udaan is a general-audience game, not a service directed specifically to children. Ad requests use Google’s general-audience content filter. We do not operate a server containing player accounts or scores. Google manages retention and privacy requests for data it processes under its own policy. If you have a concern about a child’s data, contact us through the support page.

Website and contact

This support site has no ads or analytics. GitHub Pages may process technical request data under the GitHub Privacy Statement. For questions or privacy requests, use the support page. Policy changes will be dated here.

ऐप गैर-वैयक्तिकृत विज्ञापन माँगता है। जहाँ आवश्यक हो, विज्ञापन से पहले सहमति फ़ॉर्म दिखता है। लोगो दबाकर जानकारी पैनल खोलें और उपलब्ध होने पर विज्ञापन गोपनीयता विकल्प से अपनी पसंद बदलें। नेटवर्क या सहमति में समस्या होने पर भी गेम खेल सकते हैं।

ऐप कैमरा, माइक्रोफ़ोन या सटीक लोकेशन की अनुमति नहीं माँगता। स्थानीय डेटा मिटाने के लिए ऐप हटाएँ। पूरी जानकारी ऊपर अंग्रेज़ी अनुभाग और Google की नीति में है। सवालों के लिए सहायता पेज खोलें।
