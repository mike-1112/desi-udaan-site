English

Desi Udaan is an offline arcade game. It does not create user accounts, show advertising, use analytics, track users, collect personal data, sell data, or share data with third parties.

Information stored on your device

The game stores only your selected language and best score locally on your device. This information never leaves the device and can be removed by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app.

Permissions

The Android and iOS apps do not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or notifications. Android may use the device vibrator for optional gameplay feedback.

Children’s privacy

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults because it does not collect personal information from anyone.

External pages

Privacy and support links open only when you choose them. This site uses no advertising or analytics and is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary technical request information under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the effective date on this page will be updated. For questions or support, visit the Desi Udaan support page.
